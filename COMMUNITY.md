## Listing description

Browse and edit book and film covers in Obsidian. Open a dedicated cards view or embed collections in notes, with reading mode and Live Preview support. Choose a cylindrical carousel, coverflow, album rail, horizontal or vertical curved gallery, or a skewed carousel. Drag modules to reorder them and use the keyboard to browse. New users start with eight editable offline examples. Your collections and settings remain ordinary Markdown files in your vault.

No account is required. The plugin does not sync data or play audio. User-supplied remote cover URLs load from their image servers. Built-in sample covers are bundled for offline use and are excluded from the code license; attribution is in examples/SOURCES.md.
